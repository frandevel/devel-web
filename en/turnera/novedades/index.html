What's new in Turnera

What changes in Turnera, release by release, told for the people who use it at the front desk. How each thing is used is in the manual. Each card shows the version number Turnera has run since that day. Yours is at the bottom of the menu on the left, under «Version»: if it matches the one on the card, you already have everything it describes.

26 September 2026

Latest release

Version 2026.9.294-773a6fe

Clinical record: the pain and the photos of each session

  • In each progress note, and when a case is opened, you can tick “There is pain” and give it a number from 0 to 10 with a bar that goes from green to red. In the case, each progress note carries its pain in a label of the same colour, and it also appears in the PDF.
  • Each progress note takes up to three photos to follow by eye how an injury or a scar changes. From a tablet or a phone they can be taken on the spot. Turnera reduces them before uploading and strips the camera data, such as where they were taken.
  • Above the treatment, the medicines marketed in Spain are suggested as you type three letters of their name, with their active ingredient and whether they need a prescription. They come from CIMA, the AEMPS database, always up to date. Picking one adds it to the treatment, which is still free text.
  • Case PDF report: each progress note comes out in its own block, with who saw the patient, the examination, the pain and its photos, and every page says it is a confidential document. The professional can now print it too, not just the administrator; reception cannot.
  • Fixed: correcting a progress note moved its time two hours ahead.

Training centres: following each student

  • A club, an academy or a gym no longer sees anything about clinical records. Each student has their programmes, with their goal, starting point, physical assessment and training plan.
  • Inside each programme you write the session notes: what was worked on and how it went, with up to three photos.
  • The club demo now shows only that: tennis and padel programmes and session notes, with nothing clinical.

25 September 2026

Version 2026.9.289-820772f

Recommend Turnera

  • At the top, next to the language, management now has the “Recommend Turnera” button with your centre's code, ready to copy or send by WhatsApp or email.
  • For every resource the recommended centre contracts, you earn a free month, once that centre pays its first fee, up to twelve months a year. The recommended centre pays no setup fee and gets its first month free.

Balancing the till, finally without paper

  • New cash closing screen. Every day, Turnera shows what has been charged in cash and by card. You type what is in the till and what the card terminal says, and Turnera tells you at once whether the till balances or how much is missing or over.
  • If it does not balance, you can see the payments one by one until you find the one that is wrong.
  • When you press «Close till», the closing is saved in the list of tills. From there you can look up any previous day, and management can reopen a till if it needs correcting.

The agenda at a glance

  • Each appointment takes the colour of its type of treatment, and you choose those colours in Services → Agenda colours.
  • The status of the appointment (waiting, in progress, attended…) is shown in a strip on the left.
  • The past appointments that are still not charged show a red border, so none slips through.

Finding each customer faster

  • The customer number appears everywhere: agenda, checkout, lists and reports. You can also search for a customer by their number.
  • The customer's name takes you to their record from any list or screen.

Charging with fewer clicks

  • If the customer has a voucher for that treatment, the checkout suggests it on its own: the line shows 0 € and the payment method is Voucher. If you prefer to charge it another way, the voucher session is not used.
  • The ticket you were preparing is not lost if you leave the checkout: when you come back, Turnera offers to recover it.
  • An information button shows the details of each service or product without leaving the checkout.
  • Fixed: tickets with products sometimes could not be charged.
  • Error notices now say what happened and what to do. If the failure is ours, the notice carries a reference so support finds it straight away.

Choosing treatments by their code

  • Each service can have a short code, such as IDEE20. In the appointment, «Quick selection» lets you tick several services by their code at once.

Products and stock

  • Selling a product takes it out of stock, and returning it adds it back.
  • Products brought in from another program arrive with their stock at zero in every centre, ready for the stock count.

Bringing data from another program

  • The service history arrives as each customer's visits, with its notes on every line, not as clinical cases.
  • Future appointments, stock, vouchers, gift cards and pending payments come across too.
  • The import screen explains, sheet by sheet, how to export each file from flowww.

Clocking in

  • The clock-in PIN has four digits and is sent on its own when you type the last one.
