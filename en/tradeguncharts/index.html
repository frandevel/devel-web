Features

What you need to trade, without the extras.

A native platform that starts fast and doesn't drag with several charts open. Every screenshot is from the application with real E-mini S&P 500 futures data.

Footprint and order flow

Each candle shows where trading happened: volume and delta at every price level, point of control and stacked imbalances. The level of detail changes with the zoom.

  • Candles, a per-level histogram or bid and ask figures, with no jumps between them
  • Delta and volume for every bar, with an ease panel (effort versus result)
  • Each candle's POC, even in the candles-only view
  • Ticks are recorded on your computer and rebuild the footprint of past candles

Live, tick by tick

The current candle builds up with every trade that comes from the market. Our own engine aggregates ticks on its own thread, so the interface doesn't slow down when the market speeds up.

  • Configurable repaint rate and a warning when data arrives late
  • Bar timer and a floating window with the lower timeframe
  • Crosshair synchronised across every chart

Trading from the chart

Place, move and cancel orders right on the chart. Stop, target and break-even are tied to the position and dragged with the mouse, with their ticks in view.

  • Limit, stop and market orders with configurable keyboard shortcuts
  • Automatic stop and target on entry, and break-even with an offset
  • Close the position in one click, with no confirmations that cost you ticks
  • Switch from the full-size contract to the micro (ES → MES) with one button
  • Several accounts at once and copy groups with a master and followers
  • Simulated account to practise with real data

Indicators built for order flow

A catalogue where each indicator is added, configured and can be repeated with other settings. All are calculated in real time without slowing the chart.

  • Absorption, Big Trades, Stacked Imbalances and delta divergences
  • CRT (Candle Range Theory) with its statistics, SMC, BOS and order blocks
  • Valhalla and Drakaris: session profiles, VAH, VAL, POC and volume nodes
  • VWAP, EMA, SMA, Bollinger Bands, FVG, Elliott waves and Key Candle

Volume profile of any stretch

Mark a range with the mouse and get its profile: value area, point of control, volume nodes, single prints and a VWAP anchored at its start.

  • Lines, rectangles, Fibonacci, long and short positions with their risk and reward
  • Alarms on lines and rectangles, with sound and a phone notification
  • Lockable drawings with line and text colours

Options and hedging

A chain with every expiry, 0DTE included. Click the bid or the ask to build the strategy and see its profit and loss before sending anything.

  • Greeks, implied volatility and open interest by strike
  • Maximum profit and loss, break-even points and the expiry curve
  • Gamma levels on the chart and the market's gamma regime
  • Portfolio hedging with micros, simulated or with real orders

Macro calendar

Economic events with forecast, previous and actual figures, in the panel and marked on the chart.

Phone notifications

Filled and rejected orders, drawing alarms and indicator alerts, by sound and by push notification. You choose which.

Trade journal

Every trade is recorded with its result, and entries and exits can be shown on the chart.

Watchlist

Your instruments with their currency, the day's change, a mini chart and each market's status.

Several windows and monitors

One window per instrument and per monitor, side panels on other timeframes and a saved workspace.

Your data, on your machine

You connect with your own account: your credentials never pass through us. Offline, the chart is still available with the saved candles.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us before trying it.

Brokers and data

Which brokers does it work with?

With Interactive Brokers, through your own TWS or IB Gateway. The Rithmic connection, used by many prop-firm accounts, is not available yet: it is going through certification. There is also a simulated account to practise with real data.

Do I pay for market data separately?

Yes. You subscribe to data with your broker, as with any professional platform. TradegunCharts uses your subscription and doesn't resell data.

Do my credentials go through your servers?

No. The application connects directly from your computer to your broker. Our server only checks the licence, and it doesn't need to know anything about your account for that.

Which markets can I trade?

Futures (indices, metals, energy, currencies…) and their micros, plus options and stocks or ETFs with Interactive Brokers. Footprint makes the most sense in futures, where the whole market's volume is visible.

Licence and use

On how many computers can I use it?

Each licence is activated on one computer. If you change computers, release it from Help → Licence and activate it on the new one.

Does it work without an internet connection?

To trade you need a connection to your broker. Without one you can keep analysing the chart with the saved candles, and the licence keeps working for several days without being checked.

What do I need to install it?

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, macOS 11 or later (Intel and Apple Silicon Macs; the Apple Silicon version is still experimental), or 64-bit Linux based on Ubuntu (Ubuntu, Zorin OS and other derivatives). The installer includes everything it needs and updates install themselves. The application's interface is in Spanish.

Is there support?

Yes, by email at hola@devel.es. You get an answer from the person who builds it, who uses it every day to trade.
